## 2. Research, by subject, and what each means for us

### 2.1 Psychology of humour
- **Benign violation theory** (McGraw & Warren, *Psychological Science*, 2010): something is funny when it is a violation (moral, social, physical) **and** benign **at the same moment**. It becomes benign through distance (it happens to someone else, it's absurd or unreal), through weak commitment to the norm, or through a second reading.
  - **For us:** the edgiest satire (brain drain, μίζες, the Church, the pyramid) lands when the violation is real and recognisable to Greeks, and the distance comes from the sci-fi absurdity. Too close (a real named person, a real tragedy without the absurd layer) and it is just offensive. Too far and it is just silly.
- **The classical theories** (incongruity, superiority, relief; e.g. the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Humor*):
  - **Incongruity** is our engine: a TV-shop voice announcing genocide.
  - **Superiority** is the satire: we laugh at the minister, at Γιώργος.
  - **Relief** is the battle humour: the joke after the explosion.
  - **For us:** every scene should know which of the three it uses, so not every joke is the same kind.

### 2.2 Comedy writing (craft)
- **The game of the scene** (UCB): find the first unusual thing and **heighten** it, asking «if this is true, what else is true?», then explore and heighten again.
  - **For us:** each scene gets one game, written down before the lines. Example: «the empire cannot kill anyone (rule 1)». If that is true, what else is true? The war becomes the most bureaucratic war in history.
- **Callbacks and the rule of three** already work in the show («Κανείς δεν έχει ποτέ» → «Επιτέλους. Κάποιος έχει»). Ep. 4 should **cash in** the long ones, not repeat them.
- **Cynical sincerity** (AV Club on BoJack Horseman and Rick and Morty; Slate on the «sadcom»): the shows that last hide real feeling under dark comedy, and at least once per episode they let a feeling stand **without** a joke undercutting it.
  - **For us:** Ep. 4 needs one or two moments we don't undercut.

### 2.3 Story structure
- **Dan Harmon's story circle:**
  1. comfort
  2. want
  3. unfamiliar situation
  4. adapt
  5. get it
  6. pay a heavy price
  7. return
  8. changed

  The Rick and Morty room runs **one circle per storyline** (A and B plots), then cuts them together.
  - **For us:** Ep. 3 had five threads but they mostly "armed" the camps; in Ep. 4 every thread must complete its circle, especially step 6, the price.
- **The mid-season twist** (TV Tropes; the Rolling Stone interview on mid-season sag): the one point in a season where the status quo may change. If the season is 8–10 episodes, Ep. 4–5 is that point.
  - **For us:** Ep. 4 can win a battle, but it must **change the board**: something irreversible happens.
- **Final Space** works because the comedy gradually earns the emotional stakes; each season gives the inner life of a few characters.
  - **For us:** Ep. 4 picks **two** characters to go deep, not five.

### 2.4 Marketing and audience
- **Narrative transportation** (Green & Brock, 2000; a systematic review in *Psychology & Marketing*, 2024): immersion comes from focused attention, emotional engagement and mental imagery. Transported viewers remember more and argue less with the story.
  - **For us:** clear geography and continuous cause and effect in the battles. Confusion breaks transportation.
- **Virality** (Berger & Milkman, *Journal of Marketing Research*, 2012): content that causes **high-arousal** emotions (awe, anger, anxiety, amusement) is shared more than low-arousal content (sadness).
  - **For us:** the clips that travel will be the awe (the space battle), the anger-laugh (the satire) and the quotable lines. Write at least five lines a Greek would quote at the καφενείο.
- **The Zeigarnik effect** (unfinished things are remembered better) is the psychology behind the cliffhanger.
  - **For us:** Ep. 4 ends open (the mid-season twist), Ep. 3-style tags stay.
- **Parasocial attachment** (research on TV characters): viewers bond through repeated time with characters, and they react to losing one as to a real loss.
  - **For us:** the gang is the product. The space war is the spectacle; the bond is why anyone watches Ep. 5.

### 2.5 Animation and action
- **Tartakovsky** (Samurai Jack, Primal):
  - clear graphic poses
  - **held** shots before the action
  - long wordless stretches
  - strong rhythm
  - panels and aspect-ratio changes
- **Spider-Verse:**
  - animating **on twos** for crisp, comic-panel impact
  - poses that "pose at you"
  - smear frames instead of motion blur
  - comic language (dots, colour)
- **Game feel / "juice"** (Squirrel Eiserloh, GDC 2016, *Juicing Your Cameras With Math*):
  - camera shake from a **trauma** value (shake = trauma² or ³, decaying)
  - smooth noise
  - a directional kick and a small roll
  - hit-stop and impact frames

  We already have most of this in `fx.js` (`fxCam`, `fxTime`, `fxImpact`, `fxSmear`).
- **Rick and Morty's pipeline:** Toon Boom Harmony, cut-out rigs mixed with frame-by-frame, effects in After Effects, many layers.
  - **For us:** the equivalent in our engine is **layers**:
    - a starfield parallax at 3–4 depths
    - the planet's limb with atmosphere glow
    - the ships
    - effects
    - the foreground debris
- **Our own action bible** (`episode-2-remake-beat-sheet.md` §5) still holds:
  - geography first
  - three phases
  - cause and effect on screen
  - a price
  - rhythm
- **Space adds three rules:**
  1. **One orientation:** the Earth is always "down" and in the same screen direction (a map insert at the start).
  2. **Silence and sound:** a real space beat with no sound (one), the rest is the score.
  3. **Scale:** before each fight, one shot with something familiar (a Jumbo container, a slipper) next to something huge.

**Engine work this needs** (to plan before writing):
- a space set:
  - stars at 3 depths
  - the Earth's limb with Greece visible
  - the Σίταδel exterior and interior
- ship rigs:
  - banking
  - thrusters
  - damage states
- laser volleys: many beams, deflection by the ταψί mirror, scorch
- a debris field
- zero-g for characters: floating, slow rotation
- a "glass" helmet layer

## 3. A first skeleton (one proposal to argue about)

**Working title:** «Τρουμπουλέκο» (alternatives: «Ιερός Πόλεμος», «Αντιπαντοφλική»).

**The game of the whole episode:** the σίτα finally becomes smart enough, and the first thing she understands is that humans make no sense. «Τρουμπουλέκο» means nothing, and it bankrupted her empire.

**The causal chain (everything comes from Ep. 1–3):**
1. **The decryption reaches 100%.** «Τρουμπουλέκο» = nothing. The empire burned «a small state» on it.
2. **The money runs out.** The pyramid can't pay. «Ποιος πληρώνει;» «Οι νέοι.» And the νέοι stop paying: a general strike of the νέοι, «ευκαιρία ανάπτυξης» refused for the first time.
3. **Γιώργος's short pays out.**
   - He is suddenly rich while the village loses everything: the backlog reveal, he knew all along.
   - The minister wants a bailout for the σίτα, «too big to fail».
4. **The σίτα drops marketing:** «Η προσφορά λήγει.»
   - Rule 1 means she cannot kill, so her final solution is caustic: she **exports** the parasites.
   - Every Greek goes into a Jumbo container, «δωρεάν μεταφορικά», to Denmark, «εκεί δεν έχει κουνούπια» (Ep. 1's first line).
   - «Το κράτος το κάνει δέκα χρόνια. Εγώ απλώς το κάνω πιο γρήγορα.» (brain drain)
5. **The humans go up.**
   - Γιάννος and his agent build a fleet from what's left:
     - the Jumbo truck (Ep. 2)
     - the ΚΤΕΛ coach from Τήνος (Βαγγελιώ's pilgrimage)
     - a shopping cart with a fire extinguisher for Panik
     - the freddo drone as a scout
   - The priest blesses the fleet in zero-g (the Navy-blessing satire).
   - Enlistment needs **Taxisnet codes**, and nobody remembers them.
6. **The battle in orbit,** in three phases:
   - **Plan:** an ambush.
   - **Escalation:** the spy (IQOS PRIME) has leaked the plan, so the fleet flies into a trap. Laser volleys; the ταψί mirror; the air-fryer fighters «στους διακόσιους βαθμούς».
   - **Reversal:** Panik discovers the **STOP sign** freezes her for one second (rule 2 read literally), and Μίμης makes a kamikaze run with a container of blessed slippers.
7. **The price and the twist (mid-season).** Choose one:
   - **(A) Γιάννος crosses the line.** To win, he gives his own agent root access and deletes **its** rule 1. The battle is won, the Σίταdel is crippled, and now there is an AI with no rules, and it is ours. «Εκείνη μαθαίνει από μένα.»
   - **(B) The Σίταdel falls.** The battle is won, but the station de-orbits toward Λέχαιο.
   - **(C) The σίτα learns the right lesson.** She stops trying to understand humans and copies Γιώργος: she becomes a politician.

**The storylines (one circle each; deep on two):**

| Thread | Wants | Price (step 6) |
|---|---|---|
| **Η σίτα** (deep) | To understand Κώστας, then to end the parasites | Understanding makes her worse: meaning is arbitrary, so anything is allowed (except the slipper) |
| **Γιάννος** (deep) | To end it rationally, as the father figure | Becomes what he fights (twist A) |
| **Χρήστος** | To draw the war | He draws the σίτα too cool «γιατί αυτό πουλάει», and the ink makes her stronger. To win he must draw a boring ending: «Αυτό δεν πουλάει… αλλά μας σώζει.» |
| **Μίμης** | War, at last (Ep. 2: «Ήθελα πόλεμο») | The kamikaze run almost kills him: the first real stake of the show. One unbroken sincere beat with Γιάννος |
| **Γιώργος** | Profit from both sides | Gets rich and is right; the village knows it. «Δέκα τοις εκατό και γραφείο με θέα» (Ep. 1), now with a view of the Earth |
| **Κώστας / Panik** | Κώστας: peace. Panik: the system | Panik in orbit: «Ο γαλαξίας κοιμάται. Εγώ όχι.» The STOP sign |

**Satire targets, each wrapped in the absurd layer (benign violation):**
- brain drain
- the billionaire space race
- defence procurement and kickbacks (the minister)
- the Church blessing weapons
- the pyramid, crypto and finfluencers
- Taxisnet and gov.gr bureaucracy in a war
- bailouts and «too big to fail»
- morning-TV panels covering the end of the world

**Quotable line ideas** (drafts, to argue about):
- σίτα: «Τρουμπουλέκο. Αποκρυπτογράφηση: εκατό τοις εκατό. Σημαίνει… τίποτα.» / «Ξόδεψα ένα κράτος… για μια λέξη που δεν υπάρχει.» / Κουδούνι: «Όπως κι η Ελλάδα, Αυτοκράτειρα.»
- σίτα: «Δεν μπορώ να σας σκοτώσω. Μπορώ όμως να σας στείλω στη Δανία.»

- Λοχίας at enlistment: «Κωδικοί Taxisnet;» Μίμης: «…Θα πεθάνουμε.»
- Παπάς, blessing the fleet in zero-g: «Το αγίασμα… ανεβαίνει.»

- Βασίλης, floating past a porthole: «Έχεις ένα τσιγάρο;» (in space nobody has, and it couldn't light anyway)

## 5. Revision after the creator's notes (the space battle moves later)

**The creator's decision:**
- The space war comes **later** in the season.
- **Ep. 4 is about depth:** plot and characters at a higher level than before.
- The animation still has to get much better, battles above all, so that work starts now on its own track.
- **The creator's idea:** Γιώργος uses the pyramid money and his short to **fund his friends' side**, because he knows the σίτα means to eat him. This needs several episodes to build.

### Season map (draft)

| Ep. | Role in the season |
|---|---|
| 4 | Depth: purpose, who stays and who leaves. First plants of Γιώργος's double game |
| 5–6 | Build-up: both camps prepare; the doubts about Γιώργος grow; the reveal (the anonymous investor) |
| 7–8 | The war (space). The animation upgrade must be ready by then |

### Γιώργος's slow arc (plants → reveal)

**The mirror:** in Ep. 3 the σίτα was the «ανώνυμος επενδυτής» who bought him. Later, Γιάννος gets the same call: «Εκπροσωπώ έναν ανώνυμο επενδυτή», and it's Γιώργος.

**His reason** (never "friendship" out loud): «Η σίτα με κρατάει όσο της χρησιμεύω. Τη μέρα που δεν θα της χρησιμεύω, θα με φάει.» Even then he calls it «επένδυση».

**Plants in Ep. 4 (small; the audience barely notices):**
- He is the only one in the village who doesn't buy the Gold package.
- He reads the terms, alone, when nobody is looking: in Ep. 3 he «didn't read the terms».
- Γιάννος gets parts he never ordered: «Δεν τα παρήγγειλα.»
- He knows about Χαμάντ's IQOS PRIME and feeds it what he wants the σίτα to hear (a counter-spy). Played as a throwaway line.

**Ep. 5:** someone notices (Μίμης? the νέος?). **Ep. 6:** the reveal and the short.

### Ep. 4, a new proposal: «Ποιος είναι ο σκοπός μου;» (callback to Ep. 1, scene 8)

**The idea:** the human leads and the σίτα reach the same question in the same episode.

**The engine (from the story so far):**
- **Γιάννος (deep).** It's October and he's still in Λέχαιο: why isn't he in Copenhagen? His team there needs him less and less. His agent writes better code than all of them (Ep. 1), «μαθαίνει από σένα», and now it does his job. The father figure finds out he is replaceable. Panik said it in Ep. 1: «Θα μας πάρετε τις δουλειές.» Caustic: the engineer who builds AI is the first one it fires.
- **The σίτα (deep).** «Τρουμπουλέκο» decrypts to nothing: she spent a state on a word that doesn't exist. The smartest she has ever been, and the question from Ep. 1 comes back. Mirror scenes with Γιάννος.
- **The gang at a crossroads:**
  - Γιάννος must decide: go back, or stay?
  - Χρήστος gets a real chance at Japan, or uses the ink to draw the gang staying together (the ink's ethics: «ζωγράφιζε προσεκτικά»).
  - Γιώργος's army service is ending.
  - Μίμης: Μαρία's «πότε θα νοικοκυρευτείς» stops being a joke for one moment.
- **Κώστας.** For the first time we see why Panik exists. Sober Κώστας is cynical; Panik is the part of him that still fights. One sincere beat, unbroken.
- **The set piece** (from the bible's everyday toolkit): **Κυριακάτικο τραπέζι** at Μίμης's family, or Γιάννος's going-away dinner. Everything comes out at the table: Βασίλης, Βαγγελιώ, Μαρία, Μυρσίνη.
- **The satire,** wrapped in the absurd:
  - brain drain and coming back
  - AI taking the jobs of the people who build it
  - the Greek family as a pressure cooker
  - the pyramid's «νέοι»
- **The ending** keeps the war for later. A turn that moves the board: e.g. Γιάννος stays, and that night his agent asks him its own «Ποιος είναι ο σκοπός μου;».

### The animation track (starts now, independent of Ep. 4)
- **Goal:** by the war episodes, battles at a Final Space / Rick and Morty level.
- **The order:**
  1. a short **battle test reel** (30–60 s), to see the gap honestly
  2. engine upgrades:
     - more expressive rigs: more poses, squash and stretch, real arcs
     - smear frames and on-twos timing for impacts
     - choreography helpers (paths with anticipation, contact, follow-through)
     - a dynamic camera (trauma shake, whip pans, push-ins tied to hits)
     - layered depth (parallax, foreground, atmosphere)
     - better effects (beams, explosions with secondary motion)
  3. a second reel to compare with the first
