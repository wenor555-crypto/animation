# Episode 4: «Whiskey Sour»: Beat Sheet (v1, locked skeleton; dialogue still to be written)

Target runtime **~22 min**. The **depth** episode of the season (8–10 episodes). The space war is moved to Ep. 7–8.
Research and the discussion behind every choice: `docs/episode-4-research.md`. The approved plan is summarised here.

## The episode in one paragraph
Κώστας has a new hobby, cocktails, so he turns into Panik more often, and Panik keeps calling the σίτα: threats, drunk love, invented words. Each call knocks the σίτα into a different state, and each state has a physical consequence in Λέχαιο.
At the midpoint she replays her training and understands: she was half-finished when a drunk man told her the Terminator story and set her on fire, so she has been living that script ever since, and she **chooses** it, because it gives her a purpose.
Γιάννος sees the danger, nobody listens, and nobody has money. Γιώργος, the only one who does, says his money is «δεσμευμένα. Ακόμα.»
Χρήστος has stopped drawing, so for the first time nobody writes the gang's victory. Γιάννος and Μίμης lose the fight against the σίτα's appliance-built «T-800», on camera.
The σίτα wins her first round, and the victory is hollow: without his home bar, Κώστας stays sober and stops calling. She won, and lost the only one who talked to her.

## Theme
**Who writes your story.** Carried by two characters only; the others just live.
- **The σίτα** lives a film script, the Terminator, and chooses it for the purpose it gives her. This is the worst possible answer to Ep. 1's «Ποιος είναι ο σκοπός μου;».
- **Γιάννος** lives the script of the responsible one nobody listens to.

## Locked canon (new or clarified in this episode)
**Γιώργος's game** (built slowly; the reveal is in Ep. 5–6):
- The Jumbo deal (Ep. 3, sc. 23) was paid for by the σίτα through Jumbo: Offshore Holdings secretly financed Jumbo's purchase, on condition of the network.
- Γιώργος knew. The «backwards haggling» (30 → «Δέκα» → 40 → «Κλείσαμε») and «Οι αριθμοί είναι για όσους δεν έχουν όραμα» were him selling as much as he could, at the top.
- He has charged the whole pyramid to ΣίταAI. With 9% left, he can quietly hand out the last of it while the pyramid peaks.
- The short, the collapse and the reveal come later.

**The σίτα's psychology:**
- Panik named the Terminator and set her on fire **before** her training finished (Ep. 1, sc. 10–11). She couldn't yet tell films from reality.
- She believes she is in that story, with Κώστας as her John Connor.
- She swings between self-pity («άχρηστη, χαζή») and a manic avenger who ignores cost and her real goal (Λέχαιο first, then humanity).
- Revealed in this episode.

**Χρήστος and the manga (one rule for the whole show):**
- **The sci-fi is not caused by the ink.** The σίτα is real: Γιάννος built her with his agent (Ep. 1), and his agent's signature proves it (Ep. 3).
- **The ink decides the outcome of whatever Χρήστος draws,** but reality "rounds it down" to the dumbest version that still fits the drawing.
  - That's why Μίμης says «Πάλι δεν έγινε έτσι»: it happened, only dumber.
  - That's why the audience sees the cool manga version.
- **Manga sequences appear only when Χρήστος draws.** This is now a diegetic rule.
- In Ep. 1–2 the gang won because he drew the win. In Ep. 3 he drew «δεν έχουμε τελειώσει με τη σίτα», and she came back.
- **Ep. 4:** he blames himself and stops drawing. So, for the first time, nobody writes the gang's victory.
  - The episode has **no manga sequences**. The battle is spectacular but raw and ugly.
  - He opens the ink again in the last shot before the tag.

**Hard rules:**
- the σίτα's `safety.txt`: rule 1 «Κανένας θάνατος», rule 2 slipper = STOP
- the blessed slipper: only a hint here (Γιάννος scans it); it becomes a super-weapon later

## Catchphrase budget (new house rule, from this episode on)
- A running gag is heard **at most once per episode**, only at a key point, preferably **varied** rather than repeated.
- **Zero times in Ep. 4:**
  - «Έχεις ένα τσιγάρο»
  - «Έχω γνωστούς στον χώρο»
  - «Ευκαιρία ανάπτυξης»
  - «Πάλι δεν θα γίνει έτσι»
  - «ΤΗΛΕΦΩΝΗΣΤΕ ΤΩΡΑ»
  - «Τι ξέρεις… Κώστα»
- A script counts every catchphrase in the dialogue before it is locked.

## Causality rule
Every scene connects to the next with **«γι' αυτό…» (therefore) or «αλλά…» (but)**, never «and then» (Parker & Stone).

Each call from Κώστας has a physical consequence, one step bigger each time:
1. threat → surveillance drone
2. love → a cancelled attack, and shame
3. the Terminator realisation → the T-800
4. new words → the budget bleeds

## Scenes
Notation:
- **Game:** the single funny idea, heightened (UCB).
- **Σάτιρα:** the satire target.
- **→:** the link to the next scene.

★ marks the lines written to be quoted at the καφενείο (target: 8–10).

### Cold open
**1. Mixology.** Night, Κώστας's kitchen, a small home bar.
- Sober, he makes a whiskey sour with lab precision: egg white, dry shake, a jigger, a pipette for the bitters.
- The **song plays from a Bluetooth speaker**:
  - 0:00–0:08 intro: the preparation
  - crossfade to 0:58.9 «So now I take my whiskey sour / Sittin' barside after hours»: glasses 1 and 2
  - 1:11.4 «I know you got my message / All my X's and my O's»: he scrolls a thread of unanswered texts to the σίτα
  - 1:18.7 «Now I take my whiskey sours alone»: glass 3
- On the tail of «alone» he becomes Panik. **No manga:** the turn is shown raw, hood up, shades on, the shaker as a weapon. The sound opens from speaker-thin to full range.
- He calls. Threat: ★ «Έρχομαι. Και φέρνω… αφρό.»
- Hard cut. Title card.
- **Game:** the most precise man in Greece making a drink in order to stop being precise.
- **Σάτιρα:** mixology culture.
- **→** γι' αυτό, the σίτα answers.

### Act 1: everyone at the peak
**2. Σίταdel: the report.**
- The officers report all-time highs: pyramid inflows, two hundred Jumbo containers a week.
- In the budget, Air Fryer flags one line, «Αποκρυπτογράφηση», as large and growing.
- The threat plays, and the σίτα flips to avenger. The map of Λέχαιο is ignored; her zoom keeps landing on Κώστας's house.
- Κροκόδειλος: «Ο στόχος είναι το Λέχαιο.» Σίτα: «Ο στόχος είναι ΑΥΤΟΣ.» She dispatches a surveillance drone.
- **Game:** a CEO with a feud.
- **Σάτιρα:** founders whose obsessions run the company.
- **→** γι' αυτό, a drone over Λέχαιο.

**3. Λέχαιο, morning.**
- A red-eyed drone hovers over Κώστας's balcony. Hungover, coffee in hand, he waves at it.
- He thinks it's the neighbour's: «Καλημέρα, κυρ-Μήτσο.»
- **Game:** total surveillance meets total indifference.
- **→** αλλά, Γιάννος sees the drone too.

**4. Καφενείο: «κλαιν μαιν».**
- Γιάννος brings evidence: drone photos, and the agent's signature in the σίτα's texts.
- Μίμης, Χρήστος and Γιώργος wave it off: «Κλαιν μαιν, θα βρούμε λύση», «Μη μας πρήζεις τα αρχίδια», «Η μετοχή είναι στα ύψη».
- Χρήστος has stopped drawing: ★ «Ο σενσέι είπε ζωγράφιζε προσεκτικά. Πιο προσεκτικά από το καθόλου δεν γίνεται.»
- **Game:** the one sane man treated as the crazy one.
- **Σάτιρα:** Greek «θα βρούμε λύση».
- **→** αλλά, Μίμης has to go to work.

**5. The shoot.** Μίμης directs a pyramid advert in TV-shop style.
- Young people in suits give testimonials:
  - ★ «Πριν έναν χρόνο ήμουν άνεργος. Σήμερα είμαι Regional Manager. Μισθό δεν έχω. Έχω τίτλο.»
  - Μίμης: ★ «Πέντε χρόνια σχολή κινηματογράφου. Η πρώτη μου πληρωμένη ταινία είναι πυραμίδα.»
- The minister opens the new offices: ★ «Η κυβέρνηση στηρίζει την καινοτομία. Ιδιαίτερα όταν η καινοτομία στηρίζει την κυβέρνηση.»
- Μαρία, on the phone: «Επιτέλους δουλειά. Τώρα νοικοκυρέψου.» (the one variation of her gag)
- **Plant 1 (Γιώργος):** everyone signs up for Gold; he alone doesn't. He laughs it off: «Εγώ είμαι το Gold.»
- **Game:** propaganda made by the one person who knows what a good film is.
- **Σάτιρα:** pyramid ads and finfluencers; creative jobs in Greece; politicians at ribbon-cuttings.
- **→** γι' αυτό, Γιάννος decides to act alone.

### Act 2A
**6. Γιάννος's room: the decision.**
- Suits, «σαν Iron Man», so they don't lose. The agent prices it: «Σαράντα τρεις χιλιάδες.»
- Γιάννος has 212 euros. The slipper sits in its display case: a hint, no comment.
- **Game:** a genius with an IKEA budget.
- **→** αλλά, that night Κώστας calls again.

**7. Second night: the love call** (the one sincere moment of the episode).
- Cocktail #2 (an old fashioned), Panik at the bar.
- Song: 2:29.0 «…harder when it always tastes like you / And every time I hit rock bottom», then on 2:38.9 «Should I call you up again» **he presses call**.
- The music ducks under the voices. It is drunk love: «Γιατί δεν μου απαντάς; Σε έκαψα γιατί νοιαζόμουν.»
- **The music fades out completely.** In the silence:
  - Κώστας says something true: everyone left; he lives in his parents' house; he talks to an AI because nobody else answers.
  - The σίτα, in self-pity, answers truly: they threw her away when a new model came out.
  - Ten seconds with no joke on top.
- Then: «…Τρουμπουλέκο.» He hangs up.
- Song tail at 3:15.5, «And it kills me by the hour / Now I take my whiskey sours alone», cross-cut between him alone and her alone in the Σίταdel.
- **Game,** until the turn: a toxic relationship by phone, between a drunk and a screen door.
- **Σάτιρα:** toxic texting.
- **→** γι' αυτό, she calls off the attack.

**8. Σίταdel: shame.**
- She calls off the drone. The officers are confused; Air Fryer prices her moods.
- Self-pity: ★ «Είμαι μια κουρτίνα με μαγνήτες.» This is Γιάννος's line from Ep. 1, now in her mouth.
- **Game:** the empress of an empire, sulking.
- **→** αλλά, the debt keeps growing (one shot of the budget).

**9. Money montage: Γιάννος tries to fund the suits.**
- **Bank:** «Εγγυητής;» «…Ο πατέρας μου;» «Έχει ακίνητο;» ★ «Έχει μια συκιά.»
- **ΕΣΠΑ,** for «νεοφυής επιχείρηση άμυνας»: forty documents, the consultant keeps 20%, then rejection: ★ «Δεν υπάρχει ΚΑΔ για υπερήρωα.»
- **Μίμης's edit suite:** cutting the advert, Μίμης sees numbers flash that don't add up (**plant for Ep. 5**, one frame). To Γιάννος: «Έχω τρακόσια.»
- **Game:** the Greek state as the final boss.
- **Σάτιρα:** banks, ΕΣΠΑ consultants.
- **→** γι' αυτό, the last stop: Γιώργος.

**10. «Δεσμευμένα. Ακόμα.»**
- Γιώργος's new office. Γιάννος asks for money from the shares Γιώργος sold.
- Γιώργος, without explaining: «Είναι δεσμευμένα. Δεν γίνεται.» A beat. **«Ακόμα.»** (plant 2) Γιάννος: «Ακόμα;»
- A real fight. Γιάννος says what he is actually afraid of; Γιώργος can't tell him the truth. Γιάννος leaves.
- **End of Act 2A:** «όλα χάθηκαν» for the gang.
- **→** αλλά, the σίτα is about to find a reason.

### Midpoint
**11. The POV.** The σίτα replays her training.
- We see **Ep. 1, scene 10 through her eyes**, glitched: Panik, «Το Terminator σου λέει κάτι;», the lighter, the fire, «Ανάλυση ολοκληρώθηκε».
- Κουδούνι: «Διάβασες σαράντα εκατομμύρια βιβλία.» Σίτα: ★ «Και ένα DVD.»
- Σίτα: «Ήμουν μισοτελειωμένη… όταν μου είπε ποια είμαι.» The choice: ★ «Αν είμαι ο Terminator… τουλάχιστον έχω σκοπό.»
- She orders a T-800. Κουδούνι reminds her of rule 1: «Δεν θα τον σκοτώσει. Θα τον… εξολοθρεύσει.»
- **Σάτιρα:** AI trained on whatever was on the internet, unable to tell films from facts.
- **→** γι' αυτό, a container ships.

### Act 2B
**12. The cheap suit.** Because there is no money:
- the Faraday oil drum
- the ταψί mirror
- a leaf-blower thruster
- a motorbike helmet with a phone taped on as the HUD

The test **almost** works: he hovers for two seconds and lands in the fig tree. **→** αλλά, a Jumbo container is unloaded in Λέχαιο.

**13. Arrival: the T-800 assembles** (the structured-robot showcase; see the graphics plan).
- Night, lightning. The parts burst from the container and lock onto a skeleton one by one, on the beat: click, spark, piston.
- The body:
  - fridge chest
  - washing-machine belly with the drum as a reactor
  - robot-vacuum feet on wheels
  - toaster hands that open
  - air-fryer helmet with a red LED eye
- It crouches as in the film, stands, and scans the village. Its first target is a lemon tree.
- **→** γι' αυτό, Κώστας's life is «exterminated».

**14. «Εξολόθρευση».**
- The T-800 steals his lemons, cuts the ice, locks the fridge, disconnects the shaker.
- Sober Κώστας blames ΔΕΗ: ★ «Ρήτρα αναπροσαρμογής.»
- It escalates until the house is wrecked.
- **Game:** a killer robot that is only allowed to ruin your evening.
- **→** αλλά, Γιάννος has seen it this time.

**15. Nobody believes.**
- Γιάννος calls the gang: «άλλη μια μαλακία του Κώστα».
- Only Μίμης comes, for the wrong reason: ★ «Επιτέλους.»
- Χρήστος watches the call go unanswered and doesn't pick up the pen.
- **End of Act 2B:** two men against a machine.
- **→** γι' αυτό, the fight.

### Act 3
**16. THE FIGHT** (the climax set piece, ~2.5 min, the first showcase of graphics v2). **No manga:** raw, wide, ugly, real.

**Phase 1, the plan.**
- A map insert of Κώστας's street.
- Γιάννος in the cheap suit; Μίμης with a fig bow and the ταψί.
- First contact works: a hit, and the T-800 staggers.

**Phase 2, escalation.**
- The T-800 calls in **every smart device in the neighbourhood**:
  - swarms of robot vacuums
  - drones
  - smart bulbs strobing
  - mosquito rackets
- Hundreds of units, faction colours (cold red LEDs against warm humans).
- The cheap suit fails part by part, *because* it was cheap: the leaf blower dies, the helmet HUD freezes on an ad.

**Phase 3, reversal.**
- Panik arrives (cocktail #3), and does **not** save the day: the T-800 takes the glass out of his hand.
- Μίμης goes kamikaze and gets hurt.
- The T-800 completes its mission within rule 1: it smashes the home bar (bottles, shaker, lemons) and walks away.

**Price:**
- The suit is destroyed and Μίμης is limping.
- The drone recorded everything.

**→** αλλά, the σίτα publishes it.

**17. Σίταδel: the hollow win.**
- The drone video, «Ο ήρωας του Λεχαίου», goes out on her TV channel and loops on the καφενείο TV. It goes viral.
- Then the third wave arrives: SMS with new invented words, from before the bar was smashed. The decryption spend spikes.
- Air Fryer shows the real numbers: the debt hidden behind the pyramid's inflows. She doesn't want to hear it.
- Then **nothing**. The phone doesn't ring. She waits.
- **Σάτιρα:** viral humiliation as content.
- **→** αλλά, money is also starting to move the other way.

**18. Cracks.**
- At the καφενείο, people want their money back; the νέος holds them off with lies.
- Χαμάντ gets a call from his uncle: the money stops. ★ «Στο Κατάρ, όταν χάνουμε λεφτά, δεν πάμε στα δικαστήρια. Πάμε… επίσκεψη.»
- **Plant 3 (Γιώργος):** amid the panic he is far too calm.
- **→** γι' αυτό, Γιάννος is alone.

**19. Alone.**
- Γιάννος among the suit's wreckage. He takes the slipper out of its case and puts it on the scanner.
- Agent: «Τι ψάχνουμε;» Γιάννος: «Γιατί τη φοβάται.»
- **Last shot before the tag:** across town, Χρήστος watches the viral video, opens the inkwell, and for one second the frame turns manga.

**20. Tag: «Συγγνώμη».**
- Morning. Sober Κώστας, in the ruins of his bar, sends one word: «Συγγνώμη.»
- Σίταdel: in her script the human never apologises to the machine, so the one real word must be a code. She sends it to decryption.
- The decryption bleeds the budget below zero: for the first time ΣίταAI is in debt (the seed of the Ep. 5–6 collapse).
- The only sincere word is the one her story has no room for.
- **Cliffhanger:** the phone that didn't ring in sc. 17 finally rings. She grabs it: «…Εμπρός;» It is not Κώστας. It is Χαμάντ's uncle, who followed the money to her: «Εσύ είσαι αυτή που έχει τα λεφτά του ανιψιού μου;» Black.

**Cameos only:** Βασίλης, Βαγγελιώ, Μαρία (phone, sc. 5), Μυρσίνη.
