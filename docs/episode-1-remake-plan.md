# Episode 1 remake (episode1r): the upgrade plan (PROPOSAL, waiting for the creator's «go»)

Episode 1 is the canon start and the creator likes its story. So this is an **upgrade, not a rewrite**: the same beats and the same dialogue (with a few fixes), brought up to the level of the Ep. 2 remake and Ep. 3:
- picture, depth and camera
- action and effects
- voices
- continuity with Ep. 2–3

As with Ep. 2: a new folder `episode1r/` (the old `episode1/` stays untouched). On the site it is published as `episode1` (alias, like `episode2r`). HTML drafts go to the review first, and the MP4 comes only after the creator approves.

## What the audit found (contact sheets of all 20 scenes, 19 min)

| Continuity | Ep. 2–3 later rely on things Ep. 1 never shows | Plant them (below) |
