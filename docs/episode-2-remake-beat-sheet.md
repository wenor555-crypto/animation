# Episode 2 (remake): «Τηλεφωνήστε Τώρα»: Beat Sheet (DRAFT r2, for the creator's approval)

The remake of Episode 2, written after Episode 3 and aligned with Episodes 1 and 3.
Target runtime **~22 min**, in **26 scenes**. Location: **Λέχαιο**, a week after Ep. 1, still August.
The whole episode is **one day**: morning → noon → golden hour → dusk → night. Every scene says where in the day it is.

**The brief (the creator):**
- A complete rewrite, based on Episodes 1 and 3.
- Much better battle scenes: they were boring and short of special effects.
- The scenes need depth and continuity.
- Work like a studio making a Hollywood film.

## 1. What we keep, what we change

**Kept from the interview (the creator's decisions for Ep. 2):**
- the +86 calls
- Βασίλης's order and the IQOS spy as the two chains that meet
- Βαγγελιώ sent to Τήνος
- Γιώργος voted the σίτα off the board
- the νέος
- the minister
- the fig-tree bows and Γιάννος's gadgets
- a lost first battle
- the plan born from the three-way clash
- Panik vs the IQOS
- the mecha from the Jumbo truck with the church bell as helmet
- the «ΑΝΑΚΛΗΣΗ ΠΡΟΪΟΝΤΟΣ»
- the bell-tower duel
- the open ending
- Βασίλης finally gets his cigarette
- the real Jumbo truck in the tag

**What changes, and why:**

| Problem in r1 | Fix in r2 |
|---|---|
| Battles are a list of gags in a static wide shot | Every battle is a **set piece**: a clear map of the space, three phases (plan, escalation, reversal), a hero moment for each character, and a price paid |
| Few special effects | A new effects layer for the whole show (`episode1/fx.js`, see §5): particles, explosions, shockwaves, laser beams that burn, hit-stop, slow motion, camera shake, impact frames, light from every explosion and laser |
| Scenes don't connect | **Continuity threads** (§3): one day, damage that stays, objects that travel from scene to scene, and every scene ending on the thing that starts the next |
| No depth | **Arcs** (§2): each main character wants something, is tested, and changes a little. Setups early, payoffs late |
| Doesn't fit Ep. 3 | Ep. 3 depends on Ep. 2 for several things (§4). They are now built in |

## 3. Continuity threads (the glue)

- **One day.** Morning (1–8), noon heat (9–11), golden hour battle (12–15), dusk (16–20), night (21–26). The light is keyed per scene.
- **Damage stays.** After the yard battle, every later yard shot keeps the damage:
  - arrows in the walls
  - a scorch line across the yard
  - the broken gate
  - a fig tree missing branches
  - feathers everywhere
  - the vacuum stuck on the wall
- **Objects that travel:**
  - **The ταψί:** Βαγγελιώ's kitchen (5) → Γιώργος's shield (13) → blocks the crocodile (14) → dented (15) → reflects the laser (24) → Ep. 3's anti-laser mirror.
  - **The IQOS:** pocket (3) → accidents (7) → assassin (19) → the bin (19) → the «friend» with the red LED (25) → Ep. 3.
  - **The bin:** dented by Panik in Ep. 1 (3) → walks (11) → swallows the IQOS (19) → stays red (25).
  - **Βασίλης's beach chair:** delivered first (9), and he stays sitting in it, watching TV through the whole war.
  - **Χρήστος's drawing of the ταψί:** (8) → pinned on the coop wall during the plan (17) → matched frame-for-frame in the climax (24).
- **Hand-offs between scenes.** Each scene ends on an image or sound that the next one picks up:
  - a ringing phone
  - the red LED
  - the horn of the truck
  - the TV jingle
  - the church bell
- **Map of the village,** so every chase and battle reads: the yard (fig tree, coop, gate, house) · the lane · the square with the καφενείο · the church and bell tower above the rooftops · the ravine outside the village (where she lands, Ep. 3).

## 5. The action bible (how the battles are made)

**Rules for every battle:**
1. **Geography first.** An establishing shot that shows where everyone is and what they defend (a map insert for the yard battle).
2. **Three phases.** Every battle has a plan, an escalation and a reversal. Each phase has its own hero moment and ends on a turn.
3. **Cause and effect on screen.** The shot where an arrow flies is followed by the shot where it lands. Wide shots set where things are; close-ups show the hits; cutaways are reaction shots.
4. **A price.** The heroes lose something visible: the gate, the fig tree, the νέος, the arrows, their phones.
5. **Rhythm.** Fast cuts are followed by one held beat (a slow-motion moment, or silence before the storm).

**The effects layer (`episode1/fx.js`),** deterministic and drawn only from `t`, so the frame QA stays clean:

| Tool | Used for |
|---|---|
| Particle bursts (sparks, debris, smoke, embers, chips, feathers, glass, dust) | every impact |
| Explosions: flash, fireball, shockwave ring, smoke column, flying debris, and a light that hits the whole frame | air fryers, drones, the gate, the mecha |
| Laser beam: white core, red glow, flicker, charge-up particles, sparks and a **scorch mark that stays** where it hits | ΣΙΤΑ v2 |
| Electric arcs | the racket gun, the jammer |
| Magnetic field rings | the magnet cannon |
| Camera shake (trauma model), crash zoom, whip pan | impacts, reveals |
| Hit-stop (a freeze of a few frames) and slow-motion ramps | arrow hits, the kick, the ταψί |
| Impact frames (2 inverted/ink frames), speed lines, smear streaks | manga beats |
| Foreground depth (out-of-focus leaves and grass), light shafts, lens flare | depth and scale |
| Battle score: procedural drums and bass ostinato, stingers on hits | music under the set pieces |

**The four set pieces:**

| # | Set piece | Length | Signature image |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | **Η μάχη της αυλής** | ~2:30 | The gate blown open; the sky full of drones; the laser sweeping the yard and setting the fig tree on fire |
| 19 | **Panik vs IQOS** (rooftops) | ~1:30 | Slow-motion jump between two roofs, a TEREA katana against a beer can, tiles exploding under their feet |
| 20 | **Οικογενειακή συσκευασία** | ~0:50 | The Jumbo truck unfolding into a mecha, the church bell dropping onto its head |
| 24 | **Καμπαναριό** | ~1:40 | The laser at full power; the ταψί flying up the tower; the beam reflecting back into her lens |

## 6. Scene by scene

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:40)
**1. Teaser: «Δώδεκα ώρες αργότερα».** Night. Red light. The mecha's eye over the rooftops; Panik's silhouette climbing the bell tower; the laser sweeps; Γιώργος, far below, screaming with something round and shiny in his hands. Freeze-frame. Card: «12 ΩΡΕΣ ΝΩΡΙΤΕΡΑ». *(Hollywood hook; it only makes sense at 24)*

### ACT 1: «Δύο αλυσίδες» (0:40–7:00), morning
**2. Αναπάντητη.** Κώστας, sober, on the couch. +86 rings again and again. He finally answers:
- The σίτα: «Κώστα. Εγώ είμαι. Θα γυρίσω. …Και δεν τελειώσαμε.»
- Κώστας, flat: «Δεν ενδιαφέρομαι για προσφορές.» He hangs up.

Title card «ΤΗΛΕΦΩΝΗΣΤΕ ΤΩΡΑ».
*Hand-off: the next phone is a TV phone number.*

**3. Η παραγγελία (chain A).** Βασίλης's living room. The τηλεπώληση presenter sells «Η ΕΞΥΠΝΗ ΚΑΡΕΚΛΑ ΠΑΡΑΛΙΑΣ». Βασίλης dials on autopilot, and the call lands in Shenzhen: «Καλώς ήρθατε ξανά… Βασίλη. Η διεύθυνση είναι η ίδια;» He gives it slowly. «Έχεις ένα τσιγάρο;»

**4. Ο πράκτορας (chain B).** Κώστας slides the IQOS into its charger. A hidden red flicker; its report travels east, across a map, to a screen in Shenzhen. The σίτα, voice-over, reads it:
- Κώστας wakes at two
- by the third can he becomes Panik
- same address as the chair order

«…Τι σύμπτωση.» Her plan: «Η καρέκλα μένει. Και στο ίδιο δέμα… ένας στρατός.» The Jumbo Κορίνθου clerk signs for a container without looking. On a corner in Λέχαιο, the bin Panik kicked in Ep. 1, dented, blinks red.

**5. Shenzhen: Αδελφές μου.** First big effects scene, full manga. The factory in the dark. The σίτα on top of the conveyor; one clack and a wave of red LEDs runs down ten thousand racks. The three lieutenants roll out with **product cards** (a Hollywood team intro), and she gives the speech:
- «AIR FRYER · 1500W · ΕΓΓΥΗΣΗ 6 ΜΗΝΩΝ»
- «ΚΟΥΔΟΥΝΙ · ΒΛΕΠΕΙ ΤΑ ΠΑΝΤΑ»
- «ΚΡΟΚΟΔΕΙΛΟΣ · ΦΟΥΣΚΩΤΟΣ · ΑΝΘΕΚΤΙΚΟΣ»

**6. Τήνος.** The σίτα's fake prize call: «Κερδίσατε ΔΩΡΕΑΝ προσκύνημα στην Τήνο! …Και τις παντόφλες σας μαζί. Όλες.» The kitchen: the note on the fridge, two empty spots by the door. **Γιάννος:** «Για πρώτη φορά… το σπίτι δεν έχει παντόφλα.» The camera finds the one thing Βαγγελιώ left: her **ταψί**, drying on the rack. *(setup)*

**7. Γενική συνέλευση.** Γιώργος arrives with the νέος carrying his bag («ευκαιρία ανάπτυξης»). The email from ΣίταAI: «Επιστρέφω για τη γενική συνέλευση. Φέρε τις μετοχές σου.» He voted her off the board with the νέος's shares. He asks Γιάννος for «μια βοήθεια».

**8. Arc εκδίκησης.** Χρήστος sketches; the red LED of the IQOS in the corner of his page. «Είναι arc εκδίκησης.» He turns the page: he has already drawn the last page: **a ταψί, a beam, a flash**. Μίμης: «Τι είναι αυτό;» Χρήστος: «Το τέλος.» Nobody takes it seriously.

**9. Ατυχήματα.** Sober Κώστας walks to the yard; the IQOS tries to kill him three times and fails, each more elaborate (a Rube Goldberg beat with a flowerpot, a scooter and a balcony). He never notices. «Ο στόχος είναι ακίνδυνος. Περιμένω το Panik.» *Hand-off: a truck's horn, far off.*

### ACT 2A: «Άφιξη» (7:00–12:30), noon → golden hour
**10. Παράδοση.** The heat haze; the Jumbo truck squeezes through the lanes (tracking shot, wing mirrors scraping walls). «Παράδοση για Βασίλη.» He signs: «Ήρθε και το τσιγάρο;» Μίμης: «Εγώ είπα από τα Jumbo, όχι από Temu…» The door opens by itself; the chair jumps out and opens itself; Βασίλης sits and never turns around again. Red light inside the truck. «…Ήρθαν κι από τα δύο.»

**11. Ο στρατός.** Manga splash in a camera move across the lines: air fryers, vacuum-tanks, selfie-drones, massage guns, doorbells, LED strips and the inflatable crocodile. ΣΙΤΑ v2 lands at the centre: reinforced frame, double magnets, a laser whose power limit was «editable». «Το επεξεργάστηκα.» She wants Panik («Εσύ είσαι ακίνδυνος. Θέλω τον ΑΛΛΟ.») and her CEO. Γιώργος, behind Γιάννος: «…Δεν είναι εδώ.»

**12. Το χωριό πέφτει.** One long take through Λέχαιο as each device takes the next:
- the καφενείο TV
- the church bell's timer
- the ΔΕΗ pole
- the street lights

The bin walks. The σίτα on every screen: «Καλησπέρα, παράσιτα!» The bounty on Panik and «έναν πρώην CEO». *Setup: every device in the village turns to face the καφενείο TV to take its orders.*

**13. Συμβούλιο πολέμου.** In the coop, the κότες from Ep. 1 are veterans now.
- Μίμης wants war.
- Γιώργος has a thing about Australia.
- Γιώργος video-calls **the minister**: robots for votes. We see the minister.
- Γιάννος: «Προτιμώ να με κατακτήσουν οι σίτες παρά να ψηφίσω Νέα Δημοκρατία.»
- «Άρα πόλεμος. Αλλά με σχέδιο.»

**14. Οπλοστάσιο.** A training montage, «Rocky» style, with a drum build:
- fig-branch bows with washing-line strings and reed arrows
- the Bluetooth jammer from the microwave; Γιάννος's agent: «Μόνο για αμυντική χρήση.»
- the magnet cannon from the old σίτα's nine magnets
- the racket anti-aircraft gun on a broomstick
- Μίμης drills the κότες as cavalry
- Γιώργος gets **the ταψί**: «Ό,τι πιο ανθεκτικό έχουμε.»

The sun turns gold.

**15. Η μάχη της αυλής.** Set piece 1, ~2:30.
- **Geography:** a map insert of the yard. The archers in the fig tree, the jammer by the house, the magnet cannon at the coop, Γιώργος and the ταψί at the table barricade.
- **Phase 1: the calm.** The cicadas stop. Dust beyond the gate. The **crocodile blows the gate apart**: explosion, debris, shockwave.
- **Phase 2: the fight.** Four hero moments:
  - Χρήστος in slow motion: «Headshot. Σε air fryer.» The air fryer bursts into chips.
  - The racket gun zaps a drone, which spirals down smoking and crashes.
  - The magnet cannon yanks a vacuum-tank onto the house wall: ΚΛΑΝΚ, dust, the camera shakes.
  - The crocodile charges Γιώργος; he raises the ταψί. **CLANG**, sparks, and he slides back three metres. «Χτυπάει σαν αληθινός!»
- **Phase 3: the reversal.**
  - Γιάννος fires the jammer: the drones fall like flies, but so does every phone in the yard.
  - ΣΙΤΑ v2 charges the laser: particles converge, a lens flare. The beam **sweeps the yard**, leaving a scorch line; the fig tree catches fire.
  - Μίμης's cavalry charge with the κότες ends in feathers.
  - The κουδούνι snatches **the νέος**.
  - «Μέσα! Όλοι στο κοτέτσι!»
- **Through it all:** Κώστας, sober, eats his κουλούρι in the middle of the explosions and is never hit. After the battle: «…Έγινε κάτι;»

**16. Μετά τη μάχη.** A held beat of silence. Smoke, feathers settling, the fig tree smouldering, Βασίλης in his chair still watching TV. The σίτα's voice from every speaker: «Χάρηκα! Επόμενη προσφορά σε πέντε λεπτά!» The price is counted: no arrows, no phones, no νέος.

### ACT 2B: «Το σχέδιο» (12:30–17:30), dusk
**17. Το σχέδιο.** The coop, bruised. The three clash, and the plan comes from all of them:
- **Μίμης:** «Θέλει τον Panik. Της δίνουμε τον Panik.»
- **Γιάννος:** «Είναι προϊόντα τηλεπώλησης. Υπακούν σε ό,τι μοιάζει με διαφήμιση.»
- The missing piece arrives with **the νέος**: he escaped, «promoted» to Regional Manager. «Όλος ο στρατός παίρνει διαταγές από την τηλεόραση του καφενείου.»
- The plan: **bait → ανάκληση → the καφενείο TV**.
- The presenter has to be someone who lies with a smile. Everyone looks at Γιώργος. «Συμφωνώ. …Ωχ.»
- Χρήστος pins his ταψί drawing on the coop wall. Nobody looks at it.

**18. Δόλωμα.** A beer at sunset. «Έξι το απόγευμα είναι. Δεν είμαι αλκοολικός.» «Για το χωριό.» He drinks, and a manga transformation into Panik: hood, shades, speed lines. «Η πόλη… ξύπνησε.» «Χωριό είναι.» «Για μένα είναι πόλη.»

**19. Panik εναντίον IQOS.** Set piece 2, ~1:30, full manga, on the rooftops at dusk.
- The IQOS splits into holder and charger and reassembles into a tiny assassin-mecha with a TEREA katana.
- Tiles explode under their feet; a slow-motion jump across a lane.
- The katana cuts Panik's beer can in half; the beer sprays like blood in a samurai film.
- «Σε φόρτιζα κάθε βράδυ.» Its battery blinks empty. «…Σκουπίδια.»
- Kick. It spins into the bin, and the bin swallows it and burps.

**20. Οικογενειακή συσκευασία.** Set piece 3, ~0:50. The σίτα has lost her spy, and she escalates.
- The Jumbo truck unfolds into a mecha, the army clamps onto it as limbs, and the church bell drops onto its head: **ΝΤΑΝΓΚ**.
- The laser now reaches across the village.
- «Τώρα και σε… ΟΙΚΟΓΕΝΕΙΑΚΗ ΣΥΣΚΕΥΑΣΙΑ!» Χρήστος: «Πάντα φτιάχνουν μέκα από φορτηγό.»

**21. Πολιορκία του καφενείου.** All is lost.
- The gang runs to the square under fire; the mecha's footsteps shake the ground.
- Γιάννος and the νέος hold the door with the last bows, the magnet cannon, and then figs: «Τελείωσαν τα βέλη!» «Ρίξε σύκα!»
- Inside, Μίμης can't get the TV signal; the mecha's hand comes through the window.

### ACT 3: «Ανάκληση» (17:30–21:00), night
**22. Ανάκληση προϊόντος.** Μίμης directs («Ησυχία στο πλατό!»), Χρήστος holds the storyboard, and Γιώργος, in a jacket over the uniform, is the best telemarketer of his life. «ΑΝΑΚΛΗΣΗ ΠΡΟΪΟΝΤΟΣ! …Η επιστροφή είναι ΔΩΡΕΑΝ! ΤΗΛΕΦΩΝΗΣΤΕ ΤΩΡΑ!»
- The effects ballet: the mecha comes apart piece by piece, every device folds itself back into its box and marches into the Jumbo truck, and the three lieutenants salute as they go in.
- The driver: «…Έχω δελτίο αποστολής. Δεν ρωτάω.» Μίμης: «Ήθελα πόλεμο.» Γιάννος: «Αυτό ήταν πόλεμος. Με κάμερα.»
- On the bell tower, alone: «ΕΓΩ… ΔΕΝ ΕΙΜΑΙ ΑΠΟ ΑΥΤΗ ΤΗΝ ΠΑΡΤΙΔΑ!»

**23. Όλη η ισχύς.** With no army to feed, she routes all power to the laser. Now it doesn't sting; it cuts, and the red beam carves the square. «Όλη η ενέργεια… σε μένα.» Panik starts to climb.

**24. Καμπαναριό.** Set piece 4, ~1:40, the climax, and the cold open plays again in full.
- Panik climbs through the sweeping beam, which melts tiles next to his hands.
- «Γιατί εμένα;» «Γιατί ήσουν εκεί.»
- She has him cornered at the bell, charged to full.
- Below, Γιώργος runs up the stairs with **the ταψί** (his one brave act) and throws it: «ΠΙΑΣ' ΤΟ! Ό,τι πιο ανθεκτικό έχουμε!»
- Slow motion: the ταψί spinning up through the red light; Panik catches it; the beam hits it and **reflects back into her own lens**. White flash; she's burning again.
- «Κάηκα για σένα. …Ξανά.» «…Σκουπίδια.»
- The kick, with a hit-stop and an impact frame. She flies off the tower toward the dark hills.
- Χρήστος holds up his drawing from the morning. It's the same frame.
- Her voice fades out: «Θα επιστρέψω… με ΔΩΡΕΑΝ μεταφορικάαα…»

### RESOLUTION + TAG (21:00–22:00), night
**25. Νύχτα στην αυλή.** The yard in ruins. Κώστας, sober, remembers nothing. «Πού είναι το IQOS μου;» «Στον κάδο.» «…Εκεί ανήκει.» The bin's LED is still red; the bin looks at him like a dog. Βασίλης: «Έχεις ένα τσιγάρο;» Κώστας gives him one. «…Επιτέλους. Κάποιος έχει.» The Jumbo driver hands Γιώργος an invoice: «Τα μεταφορικά της επιστροφής. Είπατε δωρεάν. Άρα πληρώνει η ΣίταAI.» Γιώργος reads the number and sits down. «…Γιάννο. Λέω να κάνουμε rebrand.»

**26. Τόμος δύο / +86.** Χρήστος inks the last page of volume 2: the ταψί and the beam. Μίμης: «Πάλι δεν έγινε έτσι.» «Ναι, ναι…» A horn: a real Jumbo truck reverses into the lane. Last shots:
- a dry ravine outside the village; a red LED blinks once in the rubbish
- Κώστας's phone buzzing face down: +86. He declines.
- Black. A clack: «Τηλεφωνήστε… τώρα.»
