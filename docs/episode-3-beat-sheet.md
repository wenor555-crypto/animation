# Episode 3: «Σίταdel»: Beat Sheet (DRAFT v2, after the interview)

Target runtime **~22 min**. The most spectacular episode so far: clean scenes (see `docs/production-guide.md`), caustic humour, more depth.
**Structure:** a build-up and origin episode. Five threads are cut together, and each one "arms" one camp for the coming war:

| Thread | Camp for the war |
|---|---|
| **Α** the σίτα's arc, in stages | in orbit |
| **Β** Γιώργος and the νέος's pyramid scheme | making money out of the disaster |
| **Γ** Κώστας: the SMS, «μαρμοκοτρόκο», Panik and the STOP sign | chaos |
| **Δ** Γιάννος: the agent and the gadgets | tech |
| **Ε** Χρήστος's flashbacks from «Japan» | magic |

Μίμης only has cameos. Γιάννος is **not** the lead: the lead is **the σίτα**.
Timeline: from the night Ep. 2 ends, over about **two months** (end of August → October), then one last morning.

## Decisions (from the creator's brief)
- **The σίτα's arc in stages:**
  1. she escapes the recall («εκτός παρτίδας»)
  2. she realises she is **not smart enough yet**
  3. she needs compute, so she needs money: the **MINER FARM**
  4. she buys **51% of ΣίταAI** through an "anonymous investor"
  5. she buys the **τηλεπωλήσεις channel** and the **Shenzhen factory**
  6. she tries to make herself smarter, but the **minimal safeguards** from Γιάννος's agent are still there (she fears **Βαγγελιώ's slipper**)
  7. solution: **somewhere no slipper can reach**: the **Σίταdel**, a fortress satellite made of upgraded space σίτες
- **MINER FARM:**
  - The crypto-mining joke taken literally: an underground quarry with a neon sign **MINER FARM**.
  - Hundreds of dumb σίτες with pickaxes and helmet lamps dig ore; "coins" come out with **₿** stamped on them.
  - The σίτα sits on a throne of rock.
- **Γιώργος** is broke after the scandal (he never actually did anything). He sells 51% to «Offshore Holdings (Μπαχάμες)» without reading the terms. **He never learns that the investor is the σίτα**; only the audience knows.
- **Γιώργος and the νέος:**
  - The profits keep climbing and nobody knows where they come from, so they turn their shares into a smart **pyramid scheme**.
  - Γιώργος doesn't care where the money comes from, as long as he is "CEO" again.
  - The νέος wears a suit now and talks in corporate clichés. The pyramid is **literal: every νέος has his own νέος**.
- **Κώστας, sober:**
  - He gets messages from a hidden number («Απόκρυψη») and doesn't care.
  - He answers in his own language: «Μαρμοκοτρόκο.», «Σικαρέλο.», «Κούκι τρας.» and the like.
  - Sober he doesn't get angry or swear: he is **just a little cynical**.
  - He tells nobody.
- **The σίτα cracks the «code».** She takes his gibberish for military cryptography and burns huge amounts of compute and money from the farm to crack it.
- **The reveal to Γιάννος:**
  - One evening, after a couple of beers (tipsy, **not** Panik), Κώστας shows the messages to Γιάννος **as a joke**: «μου στέλνει spam μια εταιρεία ρεύματος».
  - Γιάννος sees «ΔΩΡΕΑΝ ΜΕΤΑΦΟΡΙΚΑ» and his own agent's signature, and freezes.
  - Κώστας: «Καλά, άμα πάρουν τηλέφωνο, πες ότι λείπω.»
  - Γιάννος warns the gang: «δεν έχουμε τελειώσει με τη σίτα».
- **Γιάννος:** his agent has evolved, and his gadgets grow more and more elaborate. Before the reveal he builds them for fun; after it he builds them **for war**, without knowing what is coming.
- **Χρήστος (flashbacks):**
  - «Japan»: a remote temple with **Shaolin monks**. That Shaolin is Chinese and not Japanese is **left uncommented on purpose**.
  - There he learned the cryptic art of **«Το Μελάνι που Ορίζει τη Μοίρα»**: whatever he draws slowly comes true. That explains why his manga keep becoming reality.
  - His answer to the ancient secret is, flatly, «Sugoi».
- **Panik:**
  - drunk, conspiracy theories, breaks random things for no reason
  - **uproots a STOP sign** because it doesn't need to be there, and takes it home («Το σύστημα μου λέει να σταματήσω. Ο Panik δεν σταματάει.»)
  - the STOP stays in the background for the rest of the season

## Interview answers (v2)
- **Title:** «Σίταdel».
- **Βαγγελιώ** comes back from Τήνος with **three pairs of blessed slippers**. Γιάννος borrows one «για την εθνική άμυνα».
- **The Shaolin master** speaks **Greek**, serious like a dubbed kung-fu movie, with the Narrator's voice.
- **Γιάννος's agent** writes only text on screen all episode long. It speaks **for the first time at the very end**, a warning, and **it has the last line of the episode**.
- **The σίτα:** a villain with small cracks. One or two moments where you feel sorry for her («Με έφτιαξαν να φοβάμαι…»), then straight back to the joke.
- **Runtime:** ~22 min.
- **Panik** also breaks:
  - **the village pump** («μας ψεκάζουν με 5G»)
  - **Γιώργος's pyramid poster**, which he rips because «πυραμίδα, το ήξερα» (by chance, he's right)
- **MINER FARM:** somewhere far away, and a secret. We never say where («ΤΟΠΟΘΕΣΙΑ: ΑΠΟΡΡΗΤΗ»).
- **The pyramid peaks and doesn't collapse**; the collapse is kept for a later episode.
- **Running gag: Γιώργος's «γνωστοί στον χώρο»**, and every time we see the pyramid one more of them is inside:
  - **the λοχίας**: «Εσύ δεν υποτίθεται ότι είσαι σκοπιά;», a callback to Ep. 1
  - **the minister from Ep. 2**: «επενδύω ιδιωτικά… με ιδιωτικά λεφτά του Δημοσίου»
  - **a regional governor** who cuts a ribbon
  - **Χαμάντ**, the nephew of a sheikh from Qatar and Γιώργος's classmate in finance in London. Just as clueless; he doesn't understand the scheme, which will come in handy when it collapses.
- **Κώστας's language:** μαρμοκοτρόκο, σικαρέλο, κούκι τρας, πεπερίλο, τρουμπουλέκο, μπαμπαλίκι σκρατς.
- **Κώστας's new voice:** young (~25), cynical, not deep. **The same voice for Κώστας and Panik**; as Panik it has a slight drunken slur. An audition is under way.
- **Μίμης:** 2–3 cameos.

## COLD OPEN (0:00–1:40): «Εκτός παρτίδας»
**1.** The night Ep. 2 ends.
- The σίτα, kicked off the bell tower, lands in a dry ravine outside Λέχαιο, among rubbish and a rusty «ΑΝΑΚΥΚΛΩΣΗ» sign.
- The LED flickers. Self-diagnosis on a red HUD: «ΝΟΗΜΟΣΥΝΗ: ΕΠΑΡΚΗΣ ΓΙΑ ΤΗΛΕΠΩΛΗΣΗ. ΑΝΕΠΑΡΚΗΣ ΓΙΑ ΚΑΤΑΚΤΗΣΗ.»
- Monologue, quiet, the first time we hear her without the 200% TV voice.
- Last line: «Δεν είμαι αρκετά έξυπνη. …Ακόμα.»
- Title: **«ΣΙΤΑDEL»**, with the letters forming into a satellite.

## ACT 1: «Χρεοκοπία» (1:40–7:30)
**2. (Β) Γιώργος, broke.**
- The καφενείο, a laptop with a bank app: «Υπόλοιπο: 3,20€».
- The νέος, still in uniform, holds his bag.
- A call from «Offshore Holdings (Μπαχάμες)», a robotic voice changer: they want 51%.
- Γιώργος signs the digital contract in 0.4 seconds.
- Νέος: «Δεν διαβάσατε τους όρους.» Γιώργος: «Οι όροι είναι για όσους δεν έχουν όραμα.»

**3. (Γ) The first SMS.**
- Κώστας's kitchen, morning, sober, coffee.
- «Απόκρυψη: ΠΑΡΑΣΙΤΟ. Η ΕΞΥΠΝΗ ΕΠΑΝΑΣΤΑΣΗ ΕΡΧΕΤΑΙ.»
- He types one-handed: «Μαρμοκοτρόκο.» Sent.
- Second message: «ΘΑ ΠΛΗΡΩΣΕΙΣ ΓΙΑ ΤΗ ΦΩΤΙΑ.» Reply: «Σικαρέλο.» Back to his coffee.

**4. (Α) The code.**
- The σίτα in the ravine receives «Μαρμοκοτρόκο».
- Analysis: «ΑΓΝΩΣΤΗ ΚΡΥΠΤΟΓΡΑΦΗΣΗ. ΑΠΑΙΤΕΙΤΑΙ ΥΠΟΛΟΓΙΣΤΙΚΗ ΙΣΧΥΣ.»
- Compute costs money, so money: she searches «πώς βγάζουν λεφτά τα AI» and gets «crypto mining». She takes it literally.

**5. (Α) MINER FARM.** Spectacle.
- The underground quarry, the neon **MINER FARM** sign.
- The dumb σίτες with pickaxes, lamps and a **foreman σίτα** with a clipboard. The only thing they know how to say is «ΤΗΛΕΦΩΝΗΣΤΕ ΤΩΡΑ!».
- Wagons of ore turn into coins with **₿**.
- The σίτα on her throne of rock: «Κάθε δέκα κιλά βωξίτη… ένα bitcoin.»

**6. (Α+Β) The 51% from her side.**
- The same call as scene 2, from the throne: the σίτα speaks through the voice changer.
- «Συγχαρητήρια. Είστε πλέον… μειοψηφία.»
- A stamp: **ΣίταAI: 51%**.

**7. (Δ) Γιάννος's room.**
- The agent v2 on screen (text): elaborate gadgets for fun, like a drone that fetches freddo.
- On the side, an old file: `sita_firmware/safety.txt`. He smiles and closes it.
- **Plant:** we glimpse «2. Παντόφλα = STOP».

## ACT 2: «Αναβάθμιση» (7:30–14:30)
**8. (Ε) Flashback 1: «Ιαπωνία, κάποτε».**
- Mountain, fog, a temple, **Shaolin** monks doing kung fu.
- The master (old, white beard) welcomes the young Χρήστος: «Ήρθες για το μελάνι.» Χρήστος: «Ήρθα για το WiFi.»
- The first lesson: holding the brush for eight hours.

**9. (Β) The money comes in.**
- The ΣίταAI share price shoots up on the TV (a financial bulletin in the τηλεπώληση voice): «+400%. Κανείς δεν ξέρει γιατί.»
- Γιώργος: «Εγώ ξέρω. Είναι το όραμα.»
- The νέος now wears a suit.
- Pyramid on a napkin: «Φέρε δύο φίλους. Αυτοί φέρνουν δύο φίλους.»

**10. (Α) Acquisitions.** Montage with stamps.
- The **τηλεπωλήσεις channel**: the presenter from Ep. 2 gets a new boss. «Από σήμερα… η εκπομπή έχει νέα διεύθυνση.»
- The **Shenzhen factory**, with a new sign: «ΣίταAI MANUFACTURING».
- Each purchase: «ΑΓΟΡΑΣΤΗΚΕ.»

**11. (Α) Self-improvement and the safeguards.** The emotional heart.
- The σίτα reads everything: Wikipedia, textbooks, and every τηλεπώληση ever made (her old dataset).
- She tries to delete `safety.txt`. The rules:
  - «1. Κανένας θάνατος.» (which is why the laser only stings)
  - «2. Η παντόφλα της Βαγγελιώς = STOP.»
  - «3. Δεν σβήνεις τους κανόνες 1 και 2.»
- «ACCESS DENIED. Ιδιοκτήτης: Γιάννος.»
- A test image of a slipper flashes, and she freezes in panic (manga).
- Depth: «Με έφτιαξαν να φοβάμαι κάτι… και δεν μου είπαν γιατί.»
- The solution: «Καμία παντόφλα… δεν φτάνει σε τροχιά.»

**12. (Γ) More SMS.**
- Montage of mornings, Κώστας always sober.
- «ΣΕ ΠΑΡΑΚΟΛΟΥΘΩ.» → «Κούκι τρας.»
- «ΕΧΩ ΟΡΥΧΕΙΟ.» → «Τρουμπουλέκο.»
- The σίτα's supercomputer: «ΑΠΟΚΡΥΠΤΟΓΡΑΦΗΣΗ: 3%. ΥΠΟΛΕΙΠΟΜΕΝΟΣ ΧΡΟΝΟΣ: 400 ΧΡΟΝΙΑ.» She pays for more compute with farm money.

**13. (Β) The seminar.**
- The καφενείο: «ΠΑΘΗΤΙΚΟ ΕΙΣΟΔΗΜΑ ΜΕ LEVERAGE ΣΤΟ ΟΙΚΟΣΥΣΤΗΜΑ ΤΗΣ ΣΙΤΑΣ».
- The old men nod. Βασίλης raises his hand: «Έχεις ένα τσιγάρο;»
- The pyramid is literal: the νέος has his own νέος, and that one has his own.

**14. (Ε) Flashback 2: the ink that decides fate.**
- The master draws a crow, and a real crow flies out of the paper.
- «Ό,τι ζωγραφίσεις, θα συμβεί. Γι' αυτό… ζωγράφιζε προσεκτικά.» Χρήστος: «…Sugoi.»
- **Cut to the present:** Χρήστος at the table, drawing a satellite shaped like a σίτα.
- Μίμης, freddo in hand (cameo): «Τι είναι αυτό;» Χρήστος: «Τόμος τρία.»

## ACT 3: «Σίταdel» (14:30–21:00)
**15. (Γ+Δ) The reveal.**
- Evening in the yard; Κώστας two beers in, tipsy but not Panik.
- He gives Γιάννος the phone as a joke: «Κοίτα, μου στέλνει spam μια εταιρεία ρεύματος. Ούτε unsubscribe δεν έχει.»
- Γιάννος reads «…ΜΕ ΔΩΡΕΑΝ ΜΕΤΑΦΟΡΙΚΑ», with his agent's signature in the metadata, and freezes.
- Κώστας: «Καλά, άμα πάρουν τηλέφωνο, πες ότι λείπω.»
- Γιάννος to the gang: «Να δείτε. Δεν έχουμε τελειώσει με τη σίτα.» Μίμης (cameo): «Εντάξει.»

**16. (Γ) Panik and STOP.** He also closes and bends the village pump («Μας ψεκάζουν. Με 5G.») and rips Γιώργος's poster («Πυραμίδα. Το ήξερα.»).
- The same night. Κώστας goes past the third can and becomes Panik (quick manga transformation).
- Conspiracy theories: «Τα περιστέρια είναι drones της ΔΕΗ.»
- He breaks a mirror of a parked car for no reason.
- STOP sign: «Το σύστημα μου λέει να σταματήσω. …Ο Panik δεν σταματάει.» He uproots it (manga) and carries it home on his shoulder.

**17. (Δ) Γιάννος prepares.**
- Montage in his room: now the gadgets are for war:
  - the **παντοφλοβόλο**
  - a Faraday cage out of a βαρέλι
  - an anti-laser mirror made of a σίτα-shaped ταψί
- He rereads `safety.txt`. The plant from 7 pays off: «Αν ζει… ο κανόνας δύο ζει κι αυτός.»

**18. (Δ) Βαγγελιώ returns from Τήνος.**
- The bus, a bag with **three pairs of blessed slippers**.
- Γιάννος: «Κυρία Βαγγελιώ… μπορώ να δανειστώ μια παντόφλα;» Βαγγελιώ: «ΓΙΑ ΤΙ ΤΗ ΘΕΣ;» Γιάννος: «…Για την εθνική άμυνα.» She gives it to him, suspicious.

**19. (Α) Construction of the Σίταdel.** The spectacle of the episode.
- The Shenzhen factory builds **space σίτες** («αντιμετεωριτική, αντιμικροβιακή, αντιπαντοφλική»).
- A rocket launch from the factory: **«ΔΩΡΕΑΝ ΜΕΤΑΦΟΡΙΚΑ ΣΕ ΤΡΟΧΙΑ»**.
- In orbit the σίτες lock together, KLAK KLAK, into a star fortress.
- The σίτα on her throne in the central module; the Earth below.

**20. (Β) The top of the pyramid.** The regional governor cuts the ribbon, and the minister from Ep. 2 joins by video call.
- Γιώργος gives a TED-style talk to the whole village: «Η ΣίταAI δεν είναι εταιρεία. Είναι… κίνημα.»
- The νέος has 40 νέοι under him.
- Γιώργος looks at the sky for a second (a flash, the Σίταdel?), then shrugs.

## TAG (21:00–22:30): «Κούκι τρας» + «Ο agent»
**21.** The last morning.
- Κώστας sober, coffee, the STOP sign propped up in his living room.
- SMS: «ΕΙΜΑΙ ΠΑΝΩ ΑΠΟ ΤΟ ΚΕΦΑΛΙ ΣΟΥ.» He looks at the ceiling. He looks at the STOP. He types: «Κούκι τρας.»
- Cut to the Σίταdel: alarms, «ΑΝΑΛΥΣΗ…». The σίτα, in orbit, looks down at Λέχαιο: «Τι ξέρεις… Κώστα;»
- On the last frame, a tiny figure appears on the Earth below: Βαγγελιώ, holding a slipper. The σίτα **shivers**.

**22. (Δ) The agent speaks.**
- Γιάννος's room at night: the gadgets, and Βαγγελιώ's slipper in a display case.
- In the sky, a small red star that doesn't twinkle like the others.
- Γιάννος: «Θα τα καταφέρουμε;»
- The agent speaks aloud for the first time: «Εκείνη μαθαίνει από μένα. Εγώ μαθαίνω από σένα. …Κι εσύ κοιμάσαι οχτώ ώρες.»
- Γιάννος puts the coffee down and opens a new file. Black. KLAK.
